## 1. Executive Summary & Root-Cause Diagnosis

A comprehensive forensic audit of all 3D rendering surfaces in the repository — the **Main 3D Universe Explorer** (`src/stars/*`), the **Interactive Solar System Explorer** (`src/components/academy/SolarSystemExplorer3D.tsx`), the **7-Layer Cosmic Zoom Visualizer** (`src/components/academy/CosmicZoomVisualizer3D.tsx`), and the **Cinematic Lesson Scene Host & Post-Processing Pipeline** (`src/components/academy/cinematic/*`, `src/lib/cosmos/cinematic/*`) — identified 8 critical root causes of camera fluctuations, zoom-out screen jumps, frame-pacing stalls, and GPU memory leaks:

## 2. Core Architecture Delivered

### 2.1 `src/lib/rendering/performance/CosmosPerformanceEngine.ts`

Provides the unified mathematical and telemetry foundation for all 3D scenes:

- **`expDampScalar`, `expDampLogRadius`, `expDampVector3`:** Frame-rate-independent exponential decay (`1 - exp(-lambda * dt)`) and log-space radius interpolation across 25 orders of magnitude without step jumps or variable-refresh-rate jitter.
- **`computeContinuousNearFar(distanceLy)`:** Smooth C1-continuous frustum clipping plane computation that prevents both close-up planetary surface clipping and far-field galactic clipping.
- **`CameraOwnershipController`:** Deterministic single-owner state machine (`GUIDED_CINEMATIC`, `USER_ORBIT_CONTROLS`, `PLANET_TRACKING`, `SCALE_TRANSITION`, `PAUSED`) with smooth blend handoff (`getBlendAlpha`) when returning from user interaction to guided camera paths.
- **`CameraStabilityMonitor`:** Real-time and headless diagnostic monitor that detects frame-to-frame angular velocity spikes, FOV step snaps, and `NaN`/`Infinity` matrix corruption.
- **`ScaleTransitionCoordinator`:** Multi-scale transition coordinator across `PLANETARY_CLOSEUP`, `SOLAR_SYSTEM`, `INTERSTELLAR`, `GALACTIC`, and `COSMOLOGICAL` regimes with hysteresis-protected opacity weights.
- **`FramePacingProfiler`:** Zero-allocation circular ring buffer computing `avgFrameMs`, `p50FrameMs`, `p95FrameMs`, `p99FrameMs`, `stutterCount` (`>33.3ms`), and `severeFreezeCount` (`>50ms`).
