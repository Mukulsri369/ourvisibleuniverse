### 1.4 Identified Rendering Bottlenecks & Architectural Issues
1. **Multiple Concurrent WebGL Contexts**:
   - When `AcademyDashboard.tsx` renders `AcademyHero.tsx` alongside a module layout (e.g., Module 06 `SolarSystemExplorer3D.tsx` + `PlanetFormationLab3D.tsx`, or Module 10 with 4 separate `*3D.tsx` components on the same page), the browser allocates **2 to 5 simultaneous WebGL contexts**. On mobile Safari/Chrome and integrated GPUs (which cap active WebGL contexts at 8–16), this risks context loss (`webglcontextlost`) and wastes GPU memory.
2. **Continuous Unthrottled `useFrame` Loops Off-Screen**:
   - Every `<Canvas>` runs `frameloop="always"` even when scrolled out of the viewport or when COSMOS narration is paused (`PAUSED_BY_STUDENT` or `ANSWERING_QUESTION`).
3. **Lack of Logarithmic Depth / Floating-Origin Scale Transitions**:
   - In `CosmicZoomVisualizer3D.tsx` (Module 01), switching between Layer 0 (Earth, $10^7\text{ m}$) and Layer 6 (Observable Universe, $8.8 \times 10^{26}\text{ m}$) unmounts one `<group>` and mounts another with a hard cut. There is no smooth cross-scale camera dolly or floating-origin exponential scale factor ($S = 10^k$).
4. **Main-Thread Procedural Buffer Allocation During Mount**:
   - Particle arrays (e.g., 3,000–4,000 `Float32Array` points in `CosmicZoomVisualizer3D.tsx` and `Nebula3DExplorer.tsx`, and 2D canvas textures in `BlackHoleVisualizer3D.tsx`) are generated synchronously inside `useMemo` on mount without pooled geometry reuse or explicit `.dispose()` cleanup on unmount.
5. **Fullscreen DOM Portal Isolation**:
   - If a student clicks fullscreen on an individual 3D canvas today, `CosmosGuidedController`, captions, activity controls, and `KnowledgeCheck` remain outside `document.fullscreenElement` and become invisible until exiting fullscreen.

## 5. Recommended Final Technology Stack

To maximize visual fidelity, scientific precision, and 60 FPS stability **without** bloating dependencies or introducing React 19 peer conflicts, the COSMOS Cinematic Learning System will standardize on:

1. **Core 3D & Scene Graph**: `three@^0.184.0` + `@react-three/fiber@^9.6.1` (existing).
2. **Staging, Camera & Instancing Helpers**: `@react-three/drei@^10.7.7` (existing — expanding usage to `<CameraControls>`, `<Instances>`, `<Detailed>`, `<Billboard>`, `<Line>`, `<CatmullRomLine>`, `<Html>`, and `<PerformanceMonitor>`).
3. **Custom GLSL Procedural Shaders (`CosmosVisualEffectsLibrary`)**:
   - Blackbody Kelvin-to-sRGB chromaticity & solar granulation shader.
   - Rayleigh/Mie atmospheric limb scattering & sharp solar terminator shader.
   - Relativistic Kerr/Schwarzschild gravitational lensing & asymmetric Doppler-beamed accretion disk shader.
   - Logarithmic spiral density-wave & King-profile star cluster instanced point shader.

### 6.1 Core Service Responsibilities & File Paths

1. **`src/lib/cosmos/cinematic/CosmosAnimationCueRegistry.ts`**:
   - Maps every `segmentId` (all 960 segments across the 172 chapters of Modules 01–24) to a deterministic `CosmosAnimationCue` record.
   - Falls back gracefully to sublesson-level and module-level choreography if a custom sub-segment override is not specified.
2. **`src/lib/cosmos/cinematic/CosmosAnimationTimeline.ts`**:
   - Maintains **two decoupled simulation clocks**:
     - `choreographyTime`: advances strictly when `NarrationController.getState().status === "playing"`. When narration is paused or interrupted by a student question, `choreographyTime` freezes at its exact value so camera sweeps, timeline reveals (e.g., core collapse or recombination), and callout highlights pause in lockstep with the voice.
     - `ambientIdleTime`: continues at a gentle, damped rate ($0.15\times$) during pauses or Q&A so stars still twinkle softly, accretion disks still shimmer, and the student can orbit the paused 3D structure to inspect it while COSMOS answers their question.
3. **`src/lib/cosmos/cinematic/CosmosCinematicCameraRig.ts`**:
   - Integrates with `Cosmos3DAdapter.getInstance()` to respect `isUserControllingCamera()`.
   - When the student drags or pinches to orbit/zoom manually, automated camera keyframe tracking yields immediately to student control (`userOverrideActive = true`) and displays a subtle **"Recenter Camera to COSMOS View"** pill button.
   - Implements **Power-Scaled Coordinates (PSC)** $(x, y, z, k)$ where $k = \log_{10}(\text{scale in meters})$, enabling continuous logarithmic camera travel from $10^0\text{ m}$ to $10^{27}\text{ m}$ without 32-bit `Float32` z-fighting or vertex jitter.
4. **`src/lib/cosmos/cinematic/CosmosSceneRegistry.ts`**:
   - Lazy-loads (`React.lazy`) the 3D scene graph corresponding to the active `sceneId` and `sublessonId`, preventing all 24 modules' 3D geometries from loading into memory simultaneously.
   - Connects existing 3D components (`CosmicZoomVisualizer3D`, `VirtualPlanetarium3D`, `LightTravelSimulation3D`, `SunEarthMoonSimulation3D`, `SolarSystemExplorer3D`, `PlanetFormationLab3D`, `SunSimulator3D`, `OrbitalMechanicsLab3D`, `StellarMuseum3D`, `StarFormationLab3D`, `StellarEvolutionLab3D`, `Nebula3DExplorer`, `ExoplanetObservatory3D`, `GalaxyAssemblyLab3D`, `BlackHoleVisualizer3D`, `CosmicStructureVisualizer3D`) and newly upgraded 3D scenes (Modules 03, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24) to a single unified `CinematicSceneProps` contract.
5. **`src/lib/cosmos/cinematic/CosmosPerformanceGovernor.ts`**:
   - Monitors frame times via `@react-three/drei`'s `<PerformanceMonitor>` and `LearningPreferenceManager` accessibility settings (`reducedMotion`).
   - Dynamically scales device pixel ratio (`dpr` between `0.75` and `1.5`), particle buffer counts ($100\%$ on desktop GPU, $50\%$ on integrated GPU, $25\%$ on mobile), and shader ray-marching steps.
6. **`src/components/academy/cinematic/CosmosCinematicPlayer.tsx`**:
   - Provides the **"MAXIMIZE LESSON / FULLSCREEN CINEMATIC MODE"** container wrapping the active 3D scene, scientific annotations, captions, interactive activity controls, practice quiz overlay, and `CosmosGuidedController`.

## 7. Deterministic Narration-to-Animation Synchronization Design

### 7.1 Exact TypeScript Schema (`CosmosAnimationCue`)
Based on our inspection of `src/lib/cosmos/cosmos-types.ts` and `src/lib/cosmos/learning-director-types.ts`, each narration segment (`<moduleId>-<sublessonId>-<sectionKey>`) maps to the following deterministic schema:

```typescript
export type EpistemicVisualBadge =
  | "observed_data"            // e.g., Gaia DR3, EHT M87*, Planck CMB, Apollo/JWST telemetry
  | "physics_simulation"       // e.g., Newtonian N-body, Kepler solver, Planck blackbody curve
  | "scale_adjusted_model"     // e.g., Schematic planetary sizes vs. orbital radii
  | "artistic_reconstruction"  // e.g., Early quark-gluon plasma, Population III hypernova
  | "open_frontier_hypothesis";// e.g., Dark matter particle candidates, Big Rip vs. Big Freeze

export interface CameraKeyframe {
  /** Normalized progress within the segment: 0.0 (start) to 1.0 (end) */
  atProgress: number;
  /** Camera position in local scene coordinates [x, y, z] */
  position: [number, number, number];
  /** Camera lookAt target in local scene coordinates [x, y, z] */
  target: [number, number, number];
  /** Optional FOV override (default 45 deg) */
  fov?: number;
  /** Optional logarithmic scale exponent (10^k meters) for cross-scale scenes */
  scaleExponentK?: number;
}

export interface CosmosAnimationCue {
  moduleId: string;
  lessonId: string;               // Matches sublessonId in LessonContentProvider
  narrationSegmentId: string;     // Exact segmentId: "${moduleId}-${lessonId}-${sectionKey}"
  sceneId: string;                // Registered scene in CosmosSceneRegistry
  animationCueId: string;         // Deterministic phase identifier (e.g., "core-collapse")
  cameraPresetId: string;         // Named camera preset
  cameraKeyframes: CameraKeyframe[];
  highlightObjectIds: string[];   // 3D mesh/group IDs to pulse or ring-highlight
  annotations: {
    id: string;
    label: string;
    formulaOrValue?: string;
    attachToObjectId?: string;
    worldPosition?: [number, number, number];
  }[];
  sceneParameters: Record<string, number | string | boolean>;
  epistemicBadge: EpistemicVisualBadge;
  scaleDisclosure?: string;       // e.g., "Planet sizes exaggerated 500x relative to orbital radii for visibility"
  transitionMode: "smooth" | "crossfade" | "logarithmic_zoom" | "instant";
  requiresInteractivePause?: boolean;
  linkedActivityId?: string;
}
```

### 7.2 Playback-Driven Timing (Never Blind Duration Timers)
- **Confirmed Segment Start**: When `NarrationController.play()` begins playing segment $S_i$, it emits the segment transition event. `CosmosCinematicDirector` looks up `CosmosAnimationCue` for `S_i.segmentId` and sets the initial deterministic scene state (`sceneParameters`, `highlightObjectIds`, `cameraKeyframes[0]`).
- **Continuous Normalized Progress ($u \in [0, 1]$)**:
  - When playing **Gemini TTS audio** (`currentAudioElement`), normalized progress is read directly from hardware audio playback:
    $$u = \text{clamp}\!\left(\frac{\text{audioElement.currentTime}}{\text{audioElement.duration}},\, 0,\, 1\right)$$
  - When using **Web Speech API fallback** (`SpeechSynthesisUtterance`), `onboundary` word-character offsets (`event.charIndex / spokenText.length`) combined with damped frame interpolation provide $u \in [0, 1]$.
  - When in **Text-Only Accessibility Mode**, the reading timer's elapsed fraction provides $u \in [0, 1]$.
- **Confirmed Segment Completion**: The animation only advances to the next cue when `NarrationCursorManager.markSegmentConfirmedCompleted` fires upon actual audio `onended` (or when the student explicitly clicks **Next Step**).

### 7.3 Pause, Interruption, Resumption & Backward Seeking Invariants
1. **Student Clicks Pause**:
   - `CosmosLearningDirector.pauseSession()` pauses `NarrationController` and sets `CosmosAnimationTimeline` mode to `PAUSED`.
   - `choreographyTime` and $u$ freeze immediately. Camera movement stops smoothly.
2. **Student Interrupts with a Voice or Typed Question**:
   - `CosmosLearningDirector.handleStudentQuestion()` transitions state to `INTERRUPTED_BY_QUESTION` $\to$ `ANSWERING_QUESTION`.
   - `CosmosCinematicDirector` pushes a `CinematicStateBookmark` onto its stack:
     ```typescript
     interface CinematicStateBookmark {
       segmentId: string;
       normalizedProgressU: number;
       cameraSnapshot: CameraViewSnapshot;
       sceneParameters: Record<string, number | string | boolean>;
     }
     ```
   - If the student's question invokes a `Cosmos3DTools` demonstration (e.g., *"Show me Jupiter's moons"* or *"Highlight the event horizon"*), the 3D scene temporarily focuses the requested object to visually support COSMOS's answer.
   - When the student clicks **Resume Guided Lesson**, `CosmosCinematicDirector` pops the `CinematicStateBookmark`, smoothly interpolates the camera and `sceneParameters` back to the exact pre-interruption state over $800\text{ ms}$, and resumes narration.
3. **Backward Seeking / Previous Step**:
   - Because every `CosmosAnimationCue` defines the complete declarative `sceneParameters` and `cameraKeyframes` for its `narrationSegmentId` (rather than relying on stateful incremental mutations), jumping backward to any earlier segment $S_{k}$ deterministically reconstructs the exact visual state of $S_{k}$ at $u = 0$.

## 9. Scientific Visual Accuracy & Epistemic Integrity Standard

To ensure every animation is a genuine scientific instrument rather than decorative sci-fi art, all 172 chapter scenes must enforce five mandatory standards:

### 9.1 Explicit Epistemic Classification Badge
Every cinematic scene displays a live **Epistemic Status Badge** in the top HUD bar, driven by `CosmosAnimationCue.epistemicBadge`:
1. **`OBSERVED ASTRONOMICAL DATA`** (Emerald badge) — Directly derived from empirical catalogs (Gaia DR3 astrometry, JPL DE440 ephemerides, Planck CMB map, EHT M87*/Sgr A* interferometry, LIGO GW150914 strain data).
2. **`PHYSICS-BASED SIMULATION`** (Cyan badge) — Governed by real-time mathematical equations solved in code (Newtonian/Velocity-Verlet N-body orbits, Kepler's equation, Planck blackbody chromaticity, Schwarzschild null geodesics, FLRW Friedmann integration).
3. **`SCALE-ADJUSTED EDUCATIONAL MODEL`** (Amber badge) — True physical mechanism where relative sizes or distances are compressed so both bodies remain visible in one viewport. **Must** display the exact compression factor in `scaleDisclosure` (e.g., *"Schematic Scale: Planetary radii magnified 450× relative to orbital distances; click 'True Scale' to view physical proportions"*).
4. **`ARTISTIC SCIENTIFIC RECONSTRUCTION`** (Violet badge) — Phenomenon not directly resolvable by current telescopes (e.g., Quark–Gluon Plasma at $t < 10^{-5}\text{ s}$, interior of a neutron star core, Population III hypernova).
5. **`OPEN FRONTIER HYPOTHESIS`** (Rose badge) — Competing theoretical extensions under active investigation (e.g., WIMP vs. Axion dark matter, Big Rip phantom energy $w < -1$).

### 9.2 Physical Lighting & Color Fidelity
- **Directional Sunlight Terminator**: In planetary scenes (Modules 01, 05, 06, 22, 23), illumination from the central star must use directional/point lighting with near-zero ambient fill on the night hemisphere so lunar phases, planetary terminators, and eclipse umbras are physically faithful.
- **Blackbody Stellar Chromaticity**: Stellar colors in Modules 07, 09, 10, 11, and 20 must be computed from effective temperature $T_{\text{eff}}$ ($\text{K}$) using Planckian locus Kelvin-to-RGB conversion:
  - $3,000\text{ K}$ (M-class) $\to$ Warm orange-red (`#ff8a47`), never oversaturated neon red.
  - $5,778\text{ K}$ (G-class Sun) $\to$ Crisp yellowish-white (`#fff4e5`), not cartoon orange.
  - $10,000\text{ K}$ (A-class Sirius/Vega) $\to$ Pure bluish-white (`#e0f2fe`).
  - $30,000\text{ K}$ (O-class) $\to$ Brilliant blue-violet (`#93c5fd`).

### 9.3 Elimination of Visual Anti-Patterns
- **No Generic Identical Starfields**: Background stars use spectral-weighted brightness and color distributions rather than uniform white noise dots.
- **No Uncontrolled Spin Speeds**: Rotational and orbital speeds must display a clear **Time-Lapse Multiplier** indicator (e.g., `Time Scale: 1 sec = 10 days` or `1 sec = 50 Myr`) and freeze cleanly when paused.
- **No Misleading Retrograde or Eclipse Diagrams**: Eclipses must show the $5.14^\circ$ lunar orbital inclination so students understand why eclipses do not occur every month; retrograde motion must show the 3D heliocentric sightline projection.

## 11. Performance Optimization Strategy

### 11.1 Single Active WebGL `<Canvas>` Policy
- **Problem Solved**: Currently, mounting multiple `*3D.tsx` components on a single module page creates multiple WebGL contexts.
- **Architecture Rule**:
  - When **Fullscreen Cinematic Mode** is active, `CosmosCinematicPlayer` mounts **one** active `<Canvas>` and pauses/unmounts background inline canvases via an `IntersectionObserver` + `isFullscreenCinematicMode` guard.
  - For inline page mode, a shared `<CosmosViewportHost>` wrapper uses `IntersectionObserver` (`rootMargin: "200px"`) to set `frameloop="never"` on any `<Canvas>` scrolled out of view, ensuring **at most 1 `<Canvas>` is actively executing `useFrame` at any moment**.

### 11.2 Geometry Instancing & Buffer Pooling
- All multi-body scenes exceeding 50 objects (Milky Way spiral arms, Globular Clusters, Asteroid/Kuiper Belts, Cosmic Web galaxies) must use `THREE.Points` with pre-allocated `Float32Array` attributes or `@react-three/drei`'s `<Instances>` (`THREE.InstancedMesh`), capping total draw calls per scene at **$< 35$ draw calls**.
- Shared unit geometries (`unitSphereHigh` $[48\times 48]$, `unitSphereMed` $[24\times 24]$, `unitSphereLow` $[12\times 12]$, `unitRing` $[64]$) are instantiated once in a singleton pool (`CosmosCinematicAssetManager`) and reused across chapters.
- Explicit resource disposal (`geometry.dispose()`, `material.dispose()`, `texture.dispose()`) is enforced on scene transition.

### 11.3 Power-Scaled Coordinates & Logarithmic Depth
- Cross-scale scenes (Modules 01, 03, 15, 18) normalize local camera coordinates inside $[-100, +100]$ world units while tracking the base-10 scale exponent $k \in [0, 27]$ (`scaleMeters = 10^k`) in a uniform/context variable.
- Camera frustum planes are kept tightly bounded (`near = 0.05`, `far = 2000`) per scale layer, eliminating depth-buffer precision loss (`z-fighting`) without requiring expensive fragment-shader `logarithmicDepthBuffer` writes on mobile GPUs.

## 13. Known Risks & Implementation Dependencies

1. **WebGL Context Exhaustion (`webglcontextlost`)**:
   - *Risk*: Mounting a new fullscreen `<Canvas>` while 2–4 inline module `<Canvas>` components remain mounted on the underlying page can exceed mobile browser WebGL limits.
   - *Mitigation*: When `CosmosCinematicPlayer` enters fullscreen mode, inline `<Canvas>` components unmount their WebGL context (or render inside a single shared `<Canvas>` portal) so only **one** WebGL context is active at any time.
2. **Audio Playback vs. Visual Simulation Desynchronization**:
   - *Risk*: Network latency when fetching Gemini TTS audio (`synthesizeSegmentAudio`) could cause the 3D camera animation to finish before the voice even starts speaking.
   - *Mitigation*: `CosmosAnimationTimeline` only starts advancing `choreographyTime` when `NarrationController` transitions from `"loading"` to `"playing"` (after `audioElement.play()` resolves), never during TTS synthesis loading.
3. **Cross-Scale Floating-Point Jitter**:
   - *Risk*: Standard 32-bit WebGL vertex coordinates jitter noticeably when camera coordinates exceed $10^6$ units (e.g., zooming from Earth's $6,371\text{ km}$ radius to the $93\text{ Gly}$ Observable Universe).
   - *Mitigation*: Enforce floating-origin **Power-Scaled Coordinates** $(x, y, z, k)$ in `CosmosCinematicCameraRig` so local scene coordinates always stay within $[-100, +100]$ around the camera focus.
4. **Student Camera Fighting**:
   - *Risk*: Automated narration camera keyframes fighting a student who is dragging `OrbitControls` to inspect a planet or black hole.
   - *Mitigation*: Bind `OrbitControls` `onStart` / `onEnd` to `Cosmos3DAdapter.setUserControllingCamera()`. When the student touches or drags the canvas, automated camera keyframe interpolation pauses immediately until the student clicks **"Recenter View"** or advances to the next sublesson.
5. **Preserving Server-Authoritative Completion & Certification Security**:
   - *Risk*: Cinematic animations must never auto-complete interactive activities (`ACTIVITY` steps) merely because the student watched the animation.
   - *Mitigation*: When a chapter reaches an `ACTIVITY` or `KNOWLEDGE_CHECK` step, `CosmosCinematicDirector` transitions the 3D scene into interactive laboratory mode, displays the activity controls inside the fullscreen drawer, and waits for genuine student parameter manipulation validated by `submitActivityOutcome` on the server.

## 14. Acceptance Criteria for the First Prototype (Prompts 02–07: Core Engine + Modules 01, 08, 14)

Before expanding scene implementation to the remaining 21 modules (Prompts 08–16), the initial prototype (Prompts 02–07 covering Core Architecture + Modules 01, 08, and 14) must pass all 10 verification gates:

1. **Fullscreen Activation & Fallback**: Clicking `MAXIMIZE LESSON` enters native browser Fullscreen (or full-viewport portal fallback) with zero WebGL context loss and preserves all COSMOS controls, captions, and activity inputs inside the fullscreen container.
2. **Deterministic Cue Synchronization**: All 22 chapters across Modules 01 (6 chapters), 08 (8 chapters), and 14 (8 chapters)—comprising 120 narration segments—transition smoothly to their exact `CosmosAnimationCue` camera preset, object highlight, and shader state only when `NarrationController` confirms playback.
3. **Lockstep Pause & Resume**: Clicking Pause pauses both voice narration and `choreographyTime` camera/timeline progression within $< 16\text{ ms}$ (1 frame) while maintaining gentle ambient shader rendering.
4. **Question Interruption & State Bookmarking**: Asking a voice or typed question during any chapter of Modules 01, 08, or 14 pauses the lesson, allows COSMOS to answer (and optionally focus a 3D object via `Cosmos3DTools`), and restores the exact pre-interruption camera pose and animation progress $u$ upon clicking **Resume**.
5. **Backward Seeking Determinism**: Clicking **Previous Step** or selecting an earlier chapter tab deterministically reconstructs that chapter's exact 3D visual state without residual artifacts from later chapters.
6. **Student Camera Sovereignty**: Dragging or zooming the 3D canvas manually immediately yields automated camera motion without snapping or fighting, and offers a one-click **Recenter View** button.
7. **Interactive Activity Handoff**: Reaching `act_01_cosmic_zoom` (Module 01), `act_08_orbital_sandbox` (Module 08), or `act_14_black_hole_lab` (Module 14) in Fullscreen Mode exposes the interactive controls directly in the fullscreen viewport and waits for real server-validated student participation before advancing.
8. **Epistemic & Scale Transparency**: Every chapter displays its accurate **Epistemic Status Badge** (`Observed Data`, `Physics Simulation`, `Scale-Adjusted Model`, etc.) and explicit scale compression notice where applicable.
9. **Performance Budget**: Sustains $\ge 55\text{ FPS}$ on standard desktop hardware and $\ge 30\text{ FPS}$ on throttled mobile profiles with **at most 1 active WebGL render loop** at any time, and respects `reducedMotion` accessibility preferences.
10. **Zero Regression in Security & Existing Tests**: All existing validation suites (`verify-cosmos-phase3.ts`, `modules-01-12-security-validation.ts`, `modules-13-24-security-validation.ts`, `completion-engine-validation.ts`, `assessment-engine-validation.ts`, `final-exam-validation.ts`) and `vite build` pass with zero errors.
