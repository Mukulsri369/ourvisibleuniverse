# COSMOS Cinematic Storyboard Quality Review Rubric & Physics Audit Gate

- **Project**: Our Visible Universe Academy (`https://ourvisibleuniverse.in`)
- **System**: COSMOS Cinematic Learning System (Prompt 02 of 18 — Section 17)
- **Scope**: 10-Dimension Evaluation Rubric (0–5 Scale), Mandatory Approval Gates & High-Risk Astrophysical Audit Protocol

## 1. The 10-Dimension Storyboard Evaluation Rubric

Every chapter storyboard (`172/172`) is evaluated across **10 mandatory dimensions** scored from `0` to `5`.

- **Mandatory Gate Rule**: A storyboard cannot be approved for implementation unless it achieves **$\ge 4 / 5$ in every single category**—with **Scientific Accuracy** and **Educational Clarity** acting as non-negotiable hard blockers.

|   #    | Dimension                             | Score `0–2` (Reject / Redesign)                                                                                                      | Score `3` (Needs Revision)                                                                 | Score `4` (Production Approved)                                                                                    | Score `5` (Reference Planetarium Standard)                                                                                           |
| :----: | :------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1**  | **Scientific Accuracy** _(Hard Gate)_ | Depicts physical misconceptions (e.g., Big Bang as explosion in space, dark matter as opaque smoke, wrong tail direction on comets). | Qualitatively plausible but lacks scale/model disclosure or uses arbitrary stellar colors. | Physically accurate geometry, Planckian colors, and explicit representation/scale disclosures.                     | Exact analytical/catalog equations drive all motion, with documented peer-reviewed references and epistemic badges.                  |
| **2**  | **Educational Clarity** _(Hard Gate)_ | Visuals are decorative filler unrelated to the spoken narration.                                                                     | Shows the object mentioned, but not the causal physical mechanism.                         | Visually demonstrates the exact cause-and-effect mechanism taught in the sublesson.                                | Unfolds complex 3D geometry step-by-step so a student immediately grasps _why_ the phenomenon occurs.                                |
| **3**  | **Visual Distinctiveness**            | Reuses a generic rotating sphere or identical starfield across chapters.                                                             | Minor parameter tweak of a previous chapter without a distinct visual hook.                | Distinct staging, cutaway, or perspective tailored specifically to this sublesson's concept.                       | Unmistakable visual signature per chapter with bespoke shader/geometric storytelling.                                                |
| **4**  | **Cinematic Composition**             | Subject is clipped, off-center, or obscured by UI overlays.                                                                          | Acceptable framing, but HUD labels collide with 3D geometry.                               | Balanced rule-of-thirds / central-75% safe-zone framing with clean depth separation.                               | Masterful foreground/midground/background depth, rim lighting, and uncluttered negative space.                                       |
| **5**  | **Camera Quality**                    | Jerky cuts, float32 jitter, clipping planes cutting into planets, or nauseating continuous spin.                                     | Functional OrbitControls, but transitions between beats feel mechanical.                   | Frame-rate-independent damped motion ($\lambda \in [2.5, 4.5]$) in an appropriate coordinate frame.                | Purposeful scientific cinematography where camera movement itself reveals the physical insight (e.g., parallax, inclination).        |
| **6**  | **Animation Storytelling**            | Static scene or single looping rotation for the entire sublesson.                                                                    | 1–2 coarse state switches that do not match the progression of the lesson.                 | Multi-beat progression (`title` $\to$ `summary` $\to$ `deepDive` $\to$ `mathOrAnalogy`) building conceptual depth. | Seamless choreographed visual narrative where every beat answers a question raised by the previous beat.                             |
| **7**  | **Narration Synchronization**         | Uses hardcoded `setTimeout` delays that drift from COSMOS speech.                                                                    | Triggers only at sublesson start without segment-level cue mapping.                        | Mapped to real `NarrationSegment.segmentId`s with deterministic pause/resume/seek behavior.                        | Continuous normalized progress ($u_{\text{beat}} \in [0, 1]$) synchronization with exact question-interrupt restoration.             |
| **8**  | **Interaction Design**                | Passive-only where an experiment is needed, or COSMOS auto-completes student tasks.                                                  | Basic toggle with unclear physical feedback.                                               | Tactile parameter controls (`slider`/`toggle`/`preset`) with immediate physical response and director integration. | Deeply intuitive manipulation of real physical variables ($e, M_\star, z, D$) with verified activity gate integration.               |
| **9**  | **Accessibility**                     | No captions, fails under `prefers-reduced-motion`, or unusable without a mouse.                                                      | Has captions, but lacks reduced-motion camera fallback or keyboard shortcuts.              | Full captions, `prefers-reduced-motion` static-keyframe dissolve mode, keyboard shortcuts, and 2D fallback.        | Complete multi-modal parity (`aria-live` beat announcements, `junior`/`explorer`/`astronomer` level adaptation, 2D lab equivalence). |
| **10** | **Performance Feasibility**           | Spawns multiple WebGL contexts, $>100\text{ MB}$ textures, or allocates objects in `useFrame`.                                       | Runs at $60\text{ FPS}$ on desktop but drops below $25\text{ FPS}$ on mobile.              | Fits within single-canvas budget ($\le 65$ draw calls, $\le 45\text{ MB}$ GPU RAM) with 3 quality tiers.           | Zero-allocation `useFrame` loop, pooled geometry/buffers, sustaining $60\text{ FPS}$ across desktop and mobile tiers.                |

## 2. High-Risk Astrophysical Scenes Requiring Mandatory Physics Review

The following **18 chapters** involve subtle relativistic, quantum, or cosmological geometry where common animations frequently introduce scientific misconceptions. Each has been audited and assigned strict mathematical constraints in its storyboard:

| Module & Chapter                                | High-Risk Concept                         | Common Misconception to Prevent                                                                    | Mandatory Storyboard Physics Constraint                                                                                                                                                        |
| :---------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **M02.2** (`lesson-2-what-are-constellations`)  | 3D Stellar Depth of Constellations        | Showing constellation stars as equidistant dots glued to a physical shell.                         | Camera must rotate $90^\circ$ laterally to reveal Orion's stars spread from $243\text{ ly}$ (Bellatrix) to $1,340\text{ ly}$ (Alnilam).                                                        |
| **M05.2** (`lesson-2-earths-orbit-and-seasons`) | Cause of Earth's Seasons                  | Depicting a wildly eccentric Earth orbit where summer happens because Earth is closer to the Sun.  | Orbit must be shown as nearly circular ($e = 0.0167$; perihelion in January!), with seasons driven by the fixed $23.44^\circ$ axial tilt and solar incidence angle $\cos\theta_z$.             |
| **M05.5** (`lesson-5-solar-eclipses`)           | Why Eclipses Don't Happen Every Month     | Drawing the Moon's orbit coplanar ($0^\circ$) with the ecliptic.                                   | Explicitly incline the lunar orbit by $5.145^\circ$ and highlight the Line of Nodes intersection required for syzygy eclipses.                                                                 |
| **M05.7** (`lesson-7-tides-and-gravity`)        | Two-Bulge Differential Tidal Field        | Showing only one water bulge on the Moon-facing side.                                              | Render differential tidal force vectors ($\propto R_\oplus / d^3$) producing both near-side and far-side equipotential bulges.                                                                 |
| **M06.6** (`sub-06` Comets)                     | Cometary Tail Orientation                 | Drawing comet tails trailing behind the comet's velocity vector like hair in the wind.             | Blue ion tail must point **strictly anti-sunward** ($\hat{r}_{\odot\to\text{comet}}$) driven by solar wind, even as the comet moves away from the Sun after perihelion.                        |
| **M08.6** (`08-06` Kepler's 2nd Law)            | Equal Areas in Equal Times                | Animating a planet at constant angular speed $\dot{\theta} = \text{const}$ on an ellipse.          | Solve Kepler's equation $M = E - e\sin E$ so speed peaks at periapsis ($v_p/v_a = (1+e)/(1-e)$) and swept area sectors have identical surface area.                                            |
| **M08.8** (`08-08` Barycenters)                 | Two-Body Center of Mass                   | Keeping the primary star 100% stationary while a massive companion orbits it.                      | Both bodies must orbit the stationary barycenter crosshair in opposite phase ($m_1 r_1 = m_2 r_2$, $\Delta\phi = \pi$).                                                                        |
| **M13.2** (`13-2-milky-way-anatomy`)            | Spiral Arm Density Waves                  | Rotating spiral arms as rigid spokes where the same stars stay trapped forever (winding dilemma).  | Show stars orbiting through compression density waves where short-lived blue O/B stars illuminate the wave crest.                                                                              |
| **M13.5 & M16.2** (Rotation Curves)             | Flat Galactic Rotation Curves             | Showing outer disk stars slowing down in observed mode, or showing dark matter as an opaque cloud. | Contrast Keplerian $v \propto r^{-1/2}$ decline against flat $v(r) \approx \text{const}$ driven by an invisible, translucent spherical NFW gravitational halo.                                 |
| **M14.3–14.5** (Black Holes)                    | Event Horizon, Photon Sphere & Accretion  | Sucking in distant stable orbits, or symmetric un-beamed accretion rings.                          | Show stable orbits at $r \ge 3\,R_s$ (ISCO), photon sphere at $1.5\,R_s$, shadow radius $2.6\,R_s$, and relativistic Doppler beaming brightening the approaching disk wing.                    |
| **M16.3** (`sub-16-3-gravitational-lensing`)    | Bullet Cluster ($1\text{E }0657-558$)     | Conflating hot X-ray intracluster gas with dark matter.                                            | Show collisional pink X-ray gas shocked and lagging in the center while collisionless blue lensing mass contours travel ahead with the galaxies.                                               |
| **M17.1 & M18.1** (Big Bang & Expansion)        | Metric Expansion of Space                 | Animating galaxies flying outward from an explosion center into empty space.                       | Use a centerless 3D comoving coordinate grid with an observer-galaxy switcher proving every galaxy sees isotropic Hubble recession.                                                            |
| **M17.4** (`m17-l4` Recombination & CMB)        | Cosmic Microwave Background Origin        | Showing the CMB coming from a single point in space.                                               | Show opaque $3,000\text{ K}$ Thomson-scattering fog clearing everywhere simultaneously at $t=380\text{ kyr}$ ($z=1090$), observed today as a surrounding spherical surface of last scattering. |
| **M18.4** (`m18-l4` Cosmological Redshift)      | Stretching Wavelengths in Flight          | Treating cosmological redshift as a simple kinematic edge-Doppler explosion.                       | Anchor a 3D sinusoidal photon wave to the expanding comoving grid so $\lambda(t) \propto a(t)$ stretches continuously during transit.                                                          |
| **M18.8** (`m18-l8` Bound vs. Expanding)        | Local Bound Systems                       | Showing Earth's orbit or the Milky Way expanding with the Hubble flow.                             | Clearly demarcate the $\sim 1\text{ Mpc}$ turnaround boundary inside which local gravity locks the Local Group, Milky Way, Solar System, and atoms at fixed size.                              |
| **M19.5–19.6** (Heliocentric & Venus Phases)    | Retrograde Motion & Galileo's Venus Proof | Failing to show _why_ pure Ptolemaic geometry cannot produce a Gibbous/Full Venus.                 | Show Ptolemaic Venus locked between Earth and Sun (crescent-only) vs. Heliocentric Venus passing behind the Sun (full phase range + $6\times$ angular size change).                            |
| **M22.4** (`sublesson-4-space-stations`)        | Microgravity in Low Earth Orbit           | Claiming astronauts float on the ISS because "there is zero gravity in space at $400\text{ km}$".  | Display gravity vector $g(408\text{ km}) = 0.885\,g_0 = 8.68\text{ m/s}^2$ and demonstrate that weightlessness results from continuous orbital free fall.                                      |
| **M23.10** (`23-10` Evidence Boundary)          | Astrobiology Claims                       | Implying life has already been confirmed on Mars, Europa, or K2-18b.                               | Enforce a strict epistemic Evidence Boundary separating verified habitable conditions/organics from unconfirmed biosignatures ($N_{\text{confirmed life}} = 1$, Earth).                        |
