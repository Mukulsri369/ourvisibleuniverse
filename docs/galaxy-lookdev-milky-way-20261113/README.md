# Milky Way look-dev evidence, 13 November 2026

Before and after sheets for the Milky Way's move to the disk layer. Details are in [the report](../MILKY_WAY_RECONSTRUCTION_20261113.md). Each sheet shows the same camera pose twice: the pre-change renderer on the left (the renderer files of `ded7df6`, served by a pinned control server) and this change on the right.

All renders come from the isolated QA page `scripts/nearby-qa/galaxy-views.html` with the production optics pipeline, in headless Edge on the workstation's GPU (AMD Radeon integrated graphics, ANGLE Direct3D 11), 1280×800 at device pixel ratio 1, reduced here to 640×400 per half. They are reconstructions: no photograph of the whole Galaxy exists, and none was used.
