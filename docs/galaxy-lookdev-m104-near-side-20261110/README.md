# M104 near side: look-dev evidence, 10 November 2026

Before and after sheets for the sourced M104 near side. Details are in [the report](../GALAXY_NEAR_SIDE_20261110.md). All images were rendered in headless Chromium with SwiftShader, so they show appearance only, not performance.

| File                                              | What it shows                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `m104-earth-before.jpg`, `m104-earth-after.jpg`   | `scripts/galaxy-lookdev/capture.mjs --galaxies m104 --views earth --software`. The reconstruction is on the left and the registered Hubble image on the right, both north up. Before the change, the dust ring crosses the bulge north of the nucleus. After it, the ring crosses south of the nucleus, as in the image.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `m104-earth-before.json`, `m104-earth-after.json` | The sheets' appearance indicators. They are rotation-invariant, so they do not register which side the lane is on, and they barely change.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `m104-crossfade-before-after.jpg`                 | `scripts/galaxy-views-capture.mjs` front pose at 1.1 disk radii, where the photograph is at 0.42 weight over the reconstruction. Before is on the left and after on the right. The QA page puts the disk normal up, which the change turns over, so the after frame was rotated 180° to put north up in both. Before, the reconstruction's misplaced lane darkens the bulge 80–140 px north of the core by 14–23%, and reconstructed light fills in the photograph's lane to the south. After, the two lanes coincide. |

The telescope image is a reduced copy of `public/textures/galaxies/m104.jpg`: Hubble mosaic opo0328a, credit NASA/ESA and the Hubble Heritage Team (STScI/AURA). Full credit lines and terms are in `public/textures/galaxies/CREDITS.md`.
