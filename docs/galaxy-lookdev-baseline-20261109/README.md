# Galaxy look-dev baseline, 9 November 2026

These are "before" sheets from `scripts/galaxy-lookdev/capture.mjs`. Each places the OVU reconstruction (left) beside the galaxy's registered telescope image, rotated north-up (right), with the appearance indicators in JSON. They were rendered in headless Chromium with SwiftShader at source `bafc5a4`, so they show appearance only, not performance. See [the report](../GALAXY_REALISM_TOOLING_20261109.md).

The telescope images are reduced copies of the files in `public/textures/galaxies/`. Their credits:

- M51, M81 and M101: ESA/Hubble and NASA.
- M104 and NGC 1300: NASA/ESA and the Hubble Heritage Team (STScI/AURA).
- M33: ESO.

Full credit lines and usage terms are in `public/textures/galaxies/CREDITS.md` and `docs/galaxy-rendering.md`.
