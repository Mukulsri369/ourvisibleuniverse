# Solar-system imagery

Sun, Mercury, Venus atmosphere, Earth day/night/cloud maps, Mars, Jupiter,
Saturn and rings, Uranus, Neptune, and Moon: Solar System Scope / INOVE.
Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Downloaded 24 October 2026. Images unchanged. Rendered with adjusted lighting,
solar luminance conversion, cloud alpha, and ocean highlights.
The source notes that some unmapped areas contain inferred terrain.

Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, Titan, Triton: NASA / USGS Astrogeology,
Voyager, Galileo and Cassini imagery. Downloaded from this distribution mirror:
https://github.com/Sanghel/solar-system-3d/tree/develop/public/textures/satellites
Mirror provenance: https://github.com/Sanghel/solar-system-3d/blob/develop/public/textures/README.md
Original data provider: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/
The distribution identifies these as NASA public-domain imagery. Original
provider downloads were inaccessible during this work; mirror provenance is
recorded explicitly. Maps may contain low-resolution or incomplete regions.

Other moons and Pluto retain deterministic illustrative surface maps, with
improved lighting. They are not presented as measured terrain. Tiny moons
retain their irregular geometry. Belt particles are an illustrative sampling,
not an asteroid catalog.
