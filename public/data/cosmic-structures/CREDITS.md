# Cosmic-structure fields: data credits

The files in this folder are derived by `scripts/cosmic-structures/build-structure-data.ts` from public survey catalogues. Positions are measured sky coordinates and redshifts; distances, depths inside groups, orientations in depth, sizes for 6dFGS galaxies and the display palette are derived or reconstructed as described in `docs/COSMIC_STRUCTURES_20261114.md`.

- **2MASS Redshift Survey (2MRS):** Huchra et al. (2012), ApJS 199, 26; VizieR J/ApJS/199/26. This research has made use of the 2MASS Redshift Survey and of the VizieR catalogue access tool, CDS, Strasbourg, France.
- **Sloan Digital Sky Survey DR17:** Abdurro'uf et al. (2022), ApJS 259, 35. Funding for the Sloan Digital Sky Survey IV has been provided by the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation, the U.S. Department of Energy Office of Science, and the Participating Institutions. SDSS-IV acknowledges support and resources from the Center for High Performance Computing at the University of Utah. The SDSS website is www.sdss4.org.
- **6dF Galaxy Survey DR3:** Jones et al. (2004), MNRAS 355, 747; Jones et al. (2009), MNRAS 399, 683; VizieR VII/259.
- **SIMBAD:** Wenger et al. (2000), A&AS 143, 9. This research has made use of the SIMBAD database, operated at CDS, Strasbourg, France.
